I'm a bioinformatician working at the intersection of genomics and software.
Most of my days are spent on sequencing data: designing analyses, building
reproducible pipelines, and helping wet-lab colleagues get from FASTQ files to
figures they can defend.

## What I work on

- **Transcriptomics**: bulk and single-cell RNA-seq, differential expression, isoform analysis
- **Genomics**: variant calling, structural variation, genome assembly QC
- **Pipelines**: Nextflow and Snakemake workflows, containers, HPC and cloud execution
- **Methods**: benchmarking tools against each other, and writing small ones when nothing fits

## Tools I use most

Python (pandas, Biopython, scanpy), R (Bioconductor, DESeq2, ggplot2), the
usual command-line suite (samtools, bcftools, bedtools, minimap2, STAR,
salmon), and Git for everything.
