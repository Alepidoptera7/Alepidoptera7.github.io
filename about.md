My days are devoted to Bioinformatics Engineering and surrounding pursuits in the computational aspects of Bioengineering. 

## Specialization

- **Biomarker Identification**: Extracting biomarker gene candidates from NGS data. 
- **Oligonucleotide Design**: Manual and computational development of Aptamer and ASO templates. 
- **Patient Record Management**: HIPAA compliant genomic record assessment, storage and transfer. 
- **Practical Bioinformatics**: Analysis of RNA-seq and WGS tool output. 
- **Pipelines**: Study-scale Nextflow and Snakemake workflows.
- **Bioinformatic Apps**: Development of multi-user apps which process and depict biomedical data.
